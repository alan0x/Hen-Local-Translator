**Status:** Product draft for legal review. Do not publish as final legal advice.

optional spoken translation. Automated speech and translation can be wrong;
customers must not rely on output as the sole basis for medical, legal, safety,
financial, or other high-stakes decisions.

The launch plan is one subscription at $49 USD per month, including unlimited
local translation on up to two active computers. A seven-day trial begins when
the first computer is activated. Taxes, currency presentation, and any lawful
price changes must be shown before purchase.

exports remain accessible.
