# Hen Local Privacy Policy — Launch Draft

**Status:** Product draft for legal review. Do not publish as final legal advice.

**Effective date:** To be set before paid beta.

Hen Local is designed to process live audio, transcription, translation, and
spoken translation on the customer's Mac. Those contents are not sent to Hen
Local's account, licensing, or billing services.

## Information we process

- Account information: email address, account identifier, and authentication
  records handled by our account provider.
- Billing information: Stripe customer, subscription, price, payment status,
  and paid-through dates. Hen Local does not receive complete card numbers.
- Device licensing information: device name, a one-way device fingerprint,
  device public key, activation and last-used times, and license status.
- Operational security information: event type, time, related account/device,
  and limited error metadata. Logs must never include audio or transcript text.
- Support information the customer chooses to provide.

## Information kept locally by default

Audio, transcripts, translations, voice output, app preferences, and detailed
usage totals remain on the customer's Mac. Usage totals show product value and
are never used to calculate subscription charges. Transcript export occurs only
at the customer's direction.

## Why we process account data

We use the minimum account data needed to authenticate customers, manage the
seven-day trial and paid subscription, enforce the two-device limit, issue
seven-day offline licenses, prevent fraud, provide support, and meet legal and
financial obligations.

## Service providers

The launch architecture uses Supabase for authentication and product records,
Stripe for billing, and GitHub Releases for software updates. Each provider
processes data under its own terms and privacy commitments. A final policy must
list the production entities, regions, and transfer safeguards.

## Retention and choices

Customers may sign out, deactivate devices, cancel through the billing portal,
and request account deletion through the published support address. Financial
records may be retained where required by law. The production retention periods
and contact address must be inserted before publication.

## Security and changes

Hen Local uses signed device licenses, restricted server credentials, and
encrypted transport. No system is perfectly secure. Material policy changes
will be communicated through the app, website, or account email.

## Contact

**Required before launch:** legal entity, postal address, privacy email, and
regional rights language (including California and other applicable regions).
