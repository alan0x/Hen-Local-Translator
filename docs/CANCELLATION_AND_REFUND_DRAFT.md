# Cancellation and Refund Policy — Launch Draft

**Status:** Product draft for legal and payment-provider review.

- Customers can cancel at any time from the Stripe Customer Portal.
- Cancellation prevents the next renewal. Service remains available through the
  current paid-through date.
- The seven-day trial begins with first device activation. If payment details
  are collected before trial completion, the checkout screen must clearly state
  when the first charge occurs.
- Monthly charges are generally non-refundable after a billing period begins,
  except where required by law or where Hen Local approves a refund for a
  verified service problem.
- Duplicate or clearly erroneous charges should be reported to the published
  support address and reviewed promptly.
- A failed renewal receives a three-day grace period. Successful payment restores
  normal status; unresolved failure may stop new translation after the grace
  period and any paid-through access ends.

Before publication, add the support address, response target, regional cooling-
off/refund rights, tax treatment, and exact Stripe checkout wording.
